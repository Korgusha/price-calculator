​Universal Recipe & Product Cost Calculator
​An automated, formula-driven costing engine built for Excel and Google Sheets. Clean, modular, and 100% macro-free.
​Who Is This For?
​This template is built for anyone who needs accurate cost of goods sold (COGS) and unit economics, whether you run a commercial kitchen or a workshop:
​• Bakeries, Cafes and Coffee Shops: Recipe costing, drink specs, pastry batches, and menu pricing.
• Catering and Meal Prep: Batch scaling, bulk ingredient cost tracking, and per-meal margins.
• Confectionery and Home Bakers: Precision costing for custom cakes, creams, and dough batches down to the gram.
• Craft and Non-Food Makers: Easily adaptable for candles, soaps, cosmetics, or handmade items (simply swap ingredients for wax, fragrance oils, jars, or containers).
• Indie Entrepreneurs: Calculate your exact break-even point and minimum viable price before launching sales.
​Core Architecture
​The workbook uses absolute-unit logic (1g / 1ml / 1pc) across two interconnected sheets to eliminate conversion errors:
​Settings — Master Catalog (500 slots)
• Store ingredients and materials with purchase package price and net volume or weight.
• Automatically derives unit cost per 1 g/ml and per 100 g/ml.
• Includes direct vendor URL storage to streamline wholesale reordering.
​Calculation — Costing Matrix (55 Recipe Cards)
• 55 modular cards arranged in an intuitive grid layout (11 columns by 5 rows).
• Supports up to 15 line items per card with real-time VLOOKUP ingredient pricing.
• Dynamic Overheads: Input customizable percentages for equipment wear (depreciation) and labor expenses (work).
• Yield Economics: Automatically calculates both full batch cost (Total Price) and single portion cost (Price per 1).
​Quick Setup (3 Steps)
​Populate Database (Settings): Add your raw ingredients, package price, and pack weight in grams or milliliters.
​Build Your Item (Calculation): Pick a recipe card, set batch size (portion), choose ingredients from the dropdown, and enter quantities.
​Apply Margins: Enter your target depreciation (%) and Work (%) rates. Check Price per 1 to define your retail markup.
